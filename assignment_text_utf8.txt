ConsultBae ù AI Automation Take-Home Assignment
Deadline: 48 hours from the time you receive this email. Late submissions are not evaluated.
Expected effort: 10-14 focused hours across the 2 days. We are not looking for perfection, we are looking
for working software and clear thinking. You WILL hit things you have never done before. That is
intentional: figuring out what you do not know is the #1 skill this job needs.
The problem
At ConsultBae we run multiple products (recruitment gigs, CBNexus, and internal automations) and each
has its own database. Your job is to merge them and automate on top, exactly the kind of work you would
do here.
You are given 3 CSV files, each from a different system, with overlapping people and inconsistent, messy
data. This data is intentionally imperfect. Part of your evaluation is how many problems you find and how
sensibly you handle them.
Task 1 ù Merge (core)
Design one clean database (SQLite/MySQL/Postgres, your choice) and write a script or pipeline that
ingests all 3 files into it. The same person appearing in multiple files must become ONE record. Note: no
single ID field is common across all files, you will need to think about how to match people.
Task 2 ù Automate with a no-code/low-code tool (core)
Build ONE working automation using n8n (free, self-hosted or cloud trial), Make, or Zapier, connected to
your data. Pick any ONE:
A flow that receives a new CSV (webhook/email/drive trigger), checks it against your database, and
sends a duplicate alert (email/Slack/webhook)
A flow with an LLM step that auto-tags each personÆs skill category (e.g. "automation-heavy", "web
dev", "data") and writes results back
Your own idea of similar scope ù surprise us
Export the flow JSON into your repo and show it running in your video. Pure-code solutions for this task
score zero, we use these tools daily and need to see you can work no-code too.
Task 3 ù Mini audio collection app (core)
We run gig projects where thousands of workers submit audio recordings. Build a miniature version: a web
page (any stack, plain HTML/JS, Streamlit, Flask, React, your call) where a person can:
Enter their name and phone number
Record audio in the browser OR upload an audio file (either is fine)
Submit ù the audio gets stored, and a record goes into your database from Task 1
For every submitted audio, your app must automatically extract and store: duration, sample rate
(kHz), bitrate, and loudness (dB). Bonus: a rough noise/quality estimate. If you have never worked with
audio before, good, neither had we. Figure it out.
Plus a second view listing all submissions with a play button and the extracted properties. Ugly is fine;
working matters. Deploy it anywhere free (Render/Railway/Streamlit Cloud/ngrok during your video) or
demo it running locally in your recording.

Stuck log (in the README): the 2-3 hardest places you got stuck, and exactly how you got unstuck,

Reply to the assignment email with the repo link + video link before the deadline.
A note on AI tools
You may use ChatGPT/Claude/Copilot, we use them daily too. But you must understand and be able to
defend every line. Shortlisted candidates will extend their own solution live on a call. In that round your
submission is the starting point, and "the AI wrote it" is an instant no.
What we score
Working end-to-end pipeline over polish. Sensible matching logic. How many planted data issues you
caught. Judgment in your stuck log and video. Speed.
